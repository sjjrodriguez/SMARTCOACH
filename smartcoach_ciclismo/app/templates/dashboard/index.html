How is this calculated?

We compare the training load of your last 7 days with your average weekly load over the 4 weeks before that. A ratio up to 1.3 is low risk, up to 1.5 is medium risk, and above 1.5 is high risk. Load is the estimated Training Stress Score (TSS) of each workout.
