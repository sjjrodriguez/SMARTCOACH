How are plan changes decided?

Moving up needs at least 14 days and 4 logged rides, at least 85% of planned rides and 95% of planned time completed, low fatigue (about 4.5/10 or less) and a healthy latest check-in.

Moving down is considered after 7 days and 3 rides if your fatigue after rides averages 7/10 or more, or your check-ins show very high fatigue or very low recovery.

Your plan never changes on its own: you decide whether to accept a suggestion, and each change starts a new 4-week cycle.

Why this level?

Weekly hours are the main factor: Advanced plans need more than about 12 hours per week, and under 6 hours the model assigns Base Conditioning so the plan stays realistic.

Resting heart rate is a proxy for fitness. A trained heart sits around 40-55 bpm, while values above 70 bpm lean toward moderate plans.

Accumulated TSS shows how much load you have already handled. Starting at 0 keeps maximum loads lower to protect knees and joints.
