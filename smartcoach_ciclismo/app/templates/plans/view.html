How is your level calculated?

This is the main factor. The model has learned that an Advanced plan requires more than 12 hours per week. If you have fewer than 6 hours, it will assign a Base Conditioning plan so you can realistically follow it.

It works as an indicator of your current "engine". A trained heart (advanced level) pumps more blood with less effort, at around 40-55 bpm. Values above 70 bpm lead the model to suggest moderate-intensity plans.

The accumulated Training Stress Score tells the model how much training load your body has already handled. A starting TSS of 0 means you are beginning from scratch, which limits the maximum loads to protect your knees and joints.
